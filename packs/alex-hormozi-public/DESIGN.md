# Design

## Purpose

Provide an auditable public-source knowledge layer for an internal agent. The
agent should apply documented frameworks to a company-specific situation, not
pretend to be the person represented by the sources.

## Separation of concerns

Public-source knowledge stays in this pack. User-provided books, notes, and
company data will be evaluated and placed in separate private packs. A future
composite pack will combine them with explicit authority and conflict rules.

## Content rule

One source-backed idea, decision pattern, or procedure per atom. Each atom must
identify its source span, verification date, confidence, and any limitations.

## Open questions

- Which source material has explicit reuse permission or an applicable license?
- Which company decisions should the agent assist with first?
- Which approvals are required before it can take any action?
