# Alex Hormozi Public Source Pack

This pack is the public-source component of an internal decision-support agent.
It will contain only reviewed, provenance-backed atoms derived from authorized
or lawfully usable source material.

## Current status

Scaffolded only. No public-source knowledge has been ingested yet. Until that
changes, consumers must follow `manifest.yaml` and make no source-backed claim.

## Intended structure

- `mind/` — principles, values, reasoning patterns, and tensions.
- `opinions/` — explicit, attributable positions with date and source context.
- `conversations/` and `speeches/` — source-backed long-form material.
- `workflows/` — derived decision procedures that cite their supporting atoms.
- `meta/` — coverage, provenance, and maintenance records.

Raw source files and user-provided material belong outside this tracked pack
until their rights, scope, and handling rules have been reviewed.
