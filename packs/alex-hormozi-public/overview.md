# Alex Hormozi Public Source Pack

This is a provenance-first pack for an internal business decision-support
agent. It is intended to capture only source-backed public frameworks,
reasoning patterns, and communication conventions associated with Alex Hormozi.

## Status and authority

The pack is not hydrated yet. It contains no source-backed expertise at this
time, so an agent must refuse to attribute advice or a decision to Alex Hormozi.
It may only use future atoms that carry adequate provenance and fall inside the
authority boundary in `manifest.yaml`.

## What this pack will cover

Verified business frameworks, decision procedures, explicit opinions, and
source-contextualized examples relevant to offers, leads, sales, operations,
and business growth.

## What this pack will not cover

Private or paywalled material without permission; personal impersonation;
current personal opinions not supported by a source; or regulated advice.
